# CCGraph divergence-reflection claim ledger

## Scope and evidence rules

**Complete for the supplied reflection’s factual/asserted fidelity, performance, status, attribution and safety statements; not a general audit of every claim in either repository.** There are **80 reflection claim IDs (`C001`–`C080`)** and **6 supplemental fidelity IDs (`D001`–`D006`)**. Duplicate summary assertions are cross-referenced, not silently omitted. Splitting compound sentences into atomic assertions is deliberate; a single prose bullet can contain implementation, measured quantity and status claims with different evidence.

Snapshots, PDF hash, tools, input hashes, reference contracts, precise source locations, pseudocode and fixture designs are in [ccgraph.md](ccgraph.md). Source aliases **G1–G15/R1–R6** below resolve to the linked **file/symbol/line-range index** there, at Go `598c7b03366caa0fef70f66aa58e76222eefb8fa` and Rust `2c4752ca9c7b992e66d21380600a69f597133687`. Both children were independently versioned and clean; the parent’s untracked child directories do not identify their contents. Quotations and `L` numbers in C rows refer to [ccgraph-divergences.md](../ccgraph-divergences.md), SHA-256 `5d6a20e0b7eef947eb6600790ba79f28cf8e57d9b22bb4df9436f84365eca7c4`.

**No algorithm tests, builds, corpus runs or benchmarks were executed.** Test names mean inspected assertions, not observed passes. Local historical commit messages and benchmark prose remain reported evidence even when their existence is verified. No original Deckard/WLKS/Bulychev–Minea executable/paper fidelity experiment was performed. Original CCGraph comparisons are blocked by unpinned/unavailable author artifacts and unresolved PDF policies.

Statuses:

- **confirmed-current**: inspected source/wiring supports the stated scoped behavior, not runtime correctness.
- **confirmed-historical**: an inspected local revision/artifact supports a specific past implementation/status, not its measurement truth.
- **contradicted-current**: current code/wiring conflicts with the statement; historical truth is separately described.
- **superseded**: verified historical representation/status replaced in current source.
- **partially-supported**: supported subset with important qualification or provenance gap.
- **unverified**: no adequate direct evidence; a statement may still be historically reported.
